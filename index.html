What we do

Three things, every week.

Belong

Youth Clubs

Drop-in for boys Friday nights, girls Sunday mornings. A safe, supervised space to be with friends.

Train

Combat Sports

Weekly BJJ, MMA, and amateur boxing with qualified coaches. Discipline, fitness, self-defence.

Learn

Homework & GCSE

Free study support during GCSE exam times, for students who can't afford private tutors. This year's dates will be announced soon.
