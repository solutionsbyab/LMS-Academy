    What we do

    Three things, every week.

    Belong

    Youth Clubs

    Drop-in for boys Friday nights, girls Sunday mornings. A safe, supervised space to be with friends.

    Train

    Combat Sports

    Weekly BJJ, MMA, and boxing with qualified coaches. Discipline, fitness, self-defence.

    Learn

    Homework & GCSE

    Free after-school study space with GCSE prep support. Quiet room, help when it's needed.

    Why we exist

    LMA Academy exists to give the young people of Leicester a safe place to grow — a positive alternative to the streets, and a community that supports their mental and physical well-being.
