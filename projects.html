Projects

Beyond the weekly programme.

The wider initiatives we run alongside our core classes — full detail is in the Impact Report.

Lives Not Knives

Anti-knife-crime initiative, launched 2023. Leicester recorded 869 knife-related serious crimes in 2019 alone, including 5 killings and 13 attempted murders — this exists because of that.

Drugs & Alcohol Prevention

Run with Spinney Hill Drugs & Addiction. 27% of UK adults report alcohol misuse — sessions pair boxing and MMA training with support groups and education.

Mentorship

One-to-one mentoring for young people who visit the academy or are signposted to us, handled confidentially by our own staff.

Ramadan Projects

Community initiatives run during Ramadan each year.

LMA Hikers

Hiking trips for young people. Most recently Mount Snowdon, in weather that left everyone soaked.

Neurodiversity Support

Delivered in partnership with local neurodiversity services.

Community Outreach

Stalls and demonstrations at fairs and festivals across Leicester, including the One Nation Park Event.

Charity Deployments

Door-to-door collections with One Nation, plus youth volunteer deployments to Malawi and Zambia.

Youth clubs, combat sports, and free homework help. Open all week, on London Street.
